Privacy

How MFL Front Office handles information used by the site.

Data used by the site

Some features may save information you provide, choices you make, and identifiers needed to keep the site working across visits or devices. This information is used to provide and maintain MFL Front Office.

Browser data

The site may keep settings, temporary state, and cached information in your browser to improve continuity and loading. You can remove browser-stored data using your browser's site-data controls.

Shared and submitted content

Information you choose to submit or share may be stored so the requested feature can work or so the service can be maintained and improved. Content intentionally shared through a public link can be viewed by people who have that link.

Public and reference data

MFL Front Office also uses public or reference information needed to provide its tools. That information is separate from information you choose to provide to the site.
